No. 74 Shrewsbury Park is situated in one of Dublin’s most desirable addresses, a quiet, exclusive gated development, conveniently located off Merrion Road. This address offers residents the opportunity to enjoy all the entertainment, sporting and recreational amenities the city has to offer, yet live nearby in a quiet, private and secluded development.

This lovely 3-bedroomed end of Terrace home is deceptively spacious, with accommodation extending to approximately 100 sq m. The property offers generous proportions with accommodation briefly comprising entrance hall with guest bathroom, utility/cloakroom, reception/living room, kitchen and dining room on the ground floor. The first floor features three bedrooms (master en-suite) and bathroom. Outside to the front is a cobble-lock driveway, with off-street parking, to the rear is a walled garden, planted with mature shrubs and trees, which is not overlooked to the rear.

Ballsbridge has excellent transport links, including Sydney Parade & Sandymount Dart Stations which are only a short walk away and many bus routes to the City Centre /Southside.

Just a short walk to Ballsbridge, Donnybrook and Sandymount villages. There are lots of amenities nearby including the Aviva Stadium, RDS Showgrounds, Fitzwilliam Tennis Club, Elm Park Golf Club, Herbert Park, St Vincent's Hospital and the Merrion Shopping Centre.

There are a wide array of Schools and Colleges nearby including nearby St Michaels College, Blackrock College, St Conleth’s College, Muckross Park, UCD and Trinity College Dublin.
